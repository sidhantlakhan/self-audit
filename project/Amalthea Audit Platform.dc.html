13 · Rules

Tone, accessibility, content rules

Tone
  • Calm, direct, practical. Indian English.
  • Short sentences. No jargon stacks.
  • ₹ with Indian grouping: ₹4.2 crore, ₹22 lakh, ₹1,50,000.
  • Unconfirmed prices read "₹— (to be confirmed)".
Accessibility
  • WCAG AA contrast throughout.
  • No orange text under 18 px on white — use Deep #C24000.
  • Visible focus ring: 3 px #E8650A, 2 px offset.
  • 44 px minimum touch targets on mobile.
Hard content rules
  • No income, revenue or profit promises.
  • No invented testimonials, logos or statistics.
  • AI text carries "Drafted by AI" until a consultant approves.
  • Every data screen names who can see the data.
